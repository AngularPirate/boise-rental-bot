## 2. Patterns

1. **Winners ride one proven loop: short-form video, then a subscription paywall.** Cal AI, Go Tall, Vix, Louis and the girlfriend-idea app all grew mainly on TikTok/IG video. Every one monetizes by subscription.
2. **Simple, narrow, slightly "boring" ideas win.** Go Tall, Pushcroll, ONCE and Vix each do one thing. None is a platform.
3. **Distribution first, build second.** The couple's app had users before it was built. Go Tall's founder posted about 10 TikToks a day for a month. Plan the video angle before the features.
4. **Speed to launch is a feature.** Vix took about 4 weeks to build, ONCE hit $20K in 83 days, and the girlfriend-idea app hit $20K in 90. Calorie apps are crowded, so speed beats polish.
5. **Paid creators beat doing it all yourself.** Cal AI and Snag paid creators for native-style videos and then turned the winners into ads. One summary gave a rough rule of $1-2K profit per 100K views. Another founder found paid TikTok ads beat influencers ($16 cost per subscriber against $30-35 lifetime value).
6. **Hard paywalls and long onboarding are common.** Reported: a no-trial hard paywall on one ~$50K/mo app, 12-15 onboarding steps before the paywall on Glowy, and 20-25% conversion from an unskippable paywall on another app. Pushcroll found a 7-day trial beat 3 days and weekly pricing beat monthly. These are single-source claims.
7. **Pricing is low and recurring.** Cal AI's monetization page cites $2.49/mo or $29.99/yr. Weekly plans appear often.
8. **Free tools plus SEO is a slower second channel.** BetterPic grew through blog posts, programmatic SEO and free tools. It had 2 founders and 20 staff, so it is not a solo path.
9. **Small retention tweaks matter.** Symmetry reportedly gained 3% retention from one screen at the end of a workout.
10. **Compounding can take years.** Foodnoms went from $3K to $50K/mo over five years. Hype stories (Cal AI) are the exception.
11. **Struggles share a cause.** Starter Story's failure pieces (a founder with 30+ failed projects, a failed kids' education app) point to building without validating an audience. I could not read the "five reasons" list.
12. **Distribution caveat.** Cal AI is the reference case but sits in the most crowded niche, and later growth was largely paid spend (reportedly over $1M/mo on ads at $5.7M monthly revenue, unverified).

## 3. Health, nutrition and AI-app specifics

- **Calorie tracking is proven and crowded.** Cal AI scaled with creators and ads, then was reportedly bought by MyFitnessPal (Inc. headline). Foodnoms survived on a platform-native niche. Broker listings show small AI calorie trackers at $6K-64K/mo (seller-reported).
- **Fitness:** Symmetry ($160K/mo, Spanish-language niche) and Maxed AI ($14K/mo) show a language or sub-niche angle works. Pushcroll shows a behavior-change hook.
- **GLP-1:** The one real data point is a tracker at $3K then $10K in its first two months, plus Medvi in telehealth. Demand is real. Medical-adjacent claims add risk.
- **Study tools / nursing:** I found no Starter Story story on a study or flashcard app. Evidence for idea 5 is absent here.
- **Voice/receipt/menu/campsite:** No matching stories found.
- **AI cost:** One related AI solo founder (Magai, outside Starter Story) reported 20% churn and heavy API cost per action. Keep per-user AI cost in mind for photo and voice logging.

## 5. Recommended 30-day playbook

Based on the patterns above, not on any GLP-1 story. Keep copy to tracking only, no medical advice.

- **Days 1-5, validate:** Post 5-10 short videos or Reddit comments showing a protein-logging demo for GLP-1 users. Collect a waitlist (the couple's app had users before building).
- **Days 5-20, build narrow:** Voice or photo to protein/hydration/side-effect log. One screen of daily totals. Skip meal plans and social features. Aim for a build of about 4 weeks max (Vix benchmark), so ship a rough v1 in under 3.
- **Onboarding/paywall:** Short quiz (medication stage, protein goal, symptoms), personalized result, then paywall. Test 7-day trial against hard paywall, weekly against annual. Free tier = limited logs per day. This is freemium, matching his goal, with a trial paywall as the test arm.
- **Days 20-30, channel:** 1-2 short videos a day (10/day is the extreme benchmark). Then pay 3-5 small creators and track cost per subscriber.
- **Metrics to decide go/no-go:** Waitlist signups, trial start rate, day-7 retention. Stop or pivot if the demo videos draw no waitlist in 2 weeks.
- **Portfolio angle:** Write up the extraction pipeline, evaluation results and funnel numbers.
