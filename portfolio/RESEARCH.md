## Patterns that apply to Olivia
1. Career changers win with **one flagship project done thoroughly**, plus their story. Not many projects.
2. **Staff experience inside a college counts.** Gretchen Cohenour moved from higher-ed staff into a community college ID role.
3. **English and writing work can be instructional design.** Chris Friend frames a writing curriculum as his headline design project.
4. College ID leaders care about **faculty certification, course standards, and rubrics** (Kim Estes).
5. In 2026, many career changers add **one small, responsible AI element.** Optional for Olivia; a faculty guide to AI in Canvas courses could fit.
