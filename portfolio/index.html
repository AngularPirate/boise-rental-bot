How I design

A simple, repeatable process

I follow the ADDIE model and start with the end in mind: what should a learner be able to do when they finish? Every project below moves through these five steps.

  1. Step 1AnalyzeTalk with faculty and students. Find the real gap.
  2. Step 2DesignWrite measurable outcomes and map activities to them.
  3. Step 3DevelopBuild in Canvas with accessible, consistent pages.
  4. Step 4ImplementPilot with real learners and support faculty at launch.
  5. Step 5EvaluateGather feedback and data, then revise.
