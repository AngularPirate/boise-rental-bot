How I design

Start with what students should be able to do

I work backward from clear learning goals, then build the activities, practice, and assessments that line up with them. I build accessibility in from the start, not as a fix at the end.

  • Clear beats clever. Students should never have to hunt for what to do next.
  • Faculty are the subject experts. My job is to help that expertise reach students.
  • Accessible design is good design for everyone.
